About Me

Professional Massage & Wellness in Stockholm


Certified Wellness Massage Therapist In 2026, I completed my professional training as a wellness massage therapist at Axelsons Institute in Sweden.

I am a professional member of Kroppsterapeuternas Yrkesförbund (Swedish Association of Body Therapists) under the category of Wellness Massage Therapist (Friskvårdsmassör).


Banyan Spa & Wellbeing Academy In 2008, I completed massage training at Banyan Spa & Wellbeing Academy in Thailand, a respected institution specialising in massage and wellness. This training provided me with a solid foundation and a deeper understanding of various massage techniques, human anatomy and the importance of creating a holistic experience for every client.

Established My Massage Business in Sweden In 2012, I started my massage business in Sweden. Since then, I have been dedicated to providing each client with a personal and caring massage experience in a calm and welcoming environment.


My Goal My goal is to help you feel relaxed, refreshed, re-energised and restored.

Helping people feel better since 2012


Whether you want to ease muscle tension, recover after exercise, reduce everyday stress or simply enjoy a moment of relaxation, each treatment is tailored to your individual needs.

Education & Professional Qualifications

My Training & Education Over the years, I have continued to expand my knowledge in massage, spa and wellness through various training programmes in Thailand and Sweden. My educational background has provided me with knowledge of different massage techniques, body treatments and spa operations.

  • 2026 Wellness Massage Practitioner – Axelsons Institute, Sweden. Training in traditional Swedish massage, focusing on relaxation, recovery, stress management and preventive wellness. Includes full-body and partial-body massage, facial massage and techniques for easing muscle tension.

  • 2019 Thai Warrior Massage - Thai Warrior Massage Academy Advanced bodywork combining acupressure, deep tissue compression, gentle twisting, stretching and joint mobilisation to release tension and promote overall well-being.

  • 2009 Spa Business Administration – Bangkok Spa Academy Training in spa management, business operations and customer service.

  • 2009 Spa & Beauty – Thammasat University Specialised training in spa treatments, skincare and beauty care.

  • 2008 Traditional Thai Massage - Banyan Spa & Wellbeing Academy Training in traditional Thai massage principles, Thai traditional medicine, anatomy and physiology.

  • 2005 Foot Reflexology – The Wat Po Traditional Medical School Training in traditional foot reflexology, including reflex point techniques, foot and calf massage, controlled pressure and proper therapist ergonomics.

  • 2005 Aromatherapy Massage – The Union Of Thai Traditional Medicine Society Training in aromatherapy massage techniques, essential oils and their therapeutic benefits.

  • 2004 Thai Massage – The Wat Po Traditional Medical School Training in traditional Thai massage, combining acupressure, stretching and body movements.

With my experience and broad educational background, I strive to provide every client with a personalised, professional and caring massage experience.
