About Me

Professional Massage & Wellness in Stockholm


I am a Certified Wellness Massage Practitioner (Certifierad Friskvårdsmassör), trained at Axelsons Institute in Sweden. I combine professional massage techniques with a personal approach to wellness, tailored to each client's individual needs. With experience in the luxurious spa environment of Banyan Tree Bangkok, studies in Spa Management, and training in Warrior Massage, I strive to provide a caring and relaxing experience.


At KB Wellness Studio, I believe true wellness is a lifestyle, not just a luxury. With a history of providing massage services in Stockholm since 2012, my goal is to create a calm and welcoming space where you can step away from everyday pressures and focus on your well-being.
